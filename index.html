AuraFlow Publisher

A personal publishing tool for the AuraFlow AI YouTube channel.

What it is

AuraFlow Publisher is a private command-line tool written and used by a single independent developer, the owner of the AuraFlow AI channel. It runs only on the developer's own computer and acts only on that one channel. It is not distributed, sold or offered to anyone else, and it has no web interface or user accounts.

What it does

  1. Reads a finished music album prepared locally: audio tracks, cover art and a text file with titles and descriptions.
  2. Renders one video per track and one full-length mix with chapters.
  3. Uses the YouTube Data API v3, authorised with OAuth 2.0 by the channel owner, to:
    • upload those videos to the AuraFlow AI channel (videos.insert),
    • set their thumbnails and update titles, descriptions and privacy (thumbnails.set, videos.update),
    • create and organise the channel's playlists (playlists.*, playlistItems.*),
    • read the authorised channel's own basic details (channels.list).

What it does not do

  • It does not access, store or analyse data from other channels or other users.
  • It does not display or embed YouTube content anywhere.
  • It does not show ads or charge for anything.
